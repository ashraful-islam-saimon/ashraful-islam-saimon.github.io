App Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Developer: Ashraful Islam, Dhaka, Bangladesh

This policy covers the Android apps published on Google Play by Ashraful Islam ("the app", "we"). It explains what information the app uses and why. We collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

  • Your Google account email, only if you choose to unlock paid content with it. We use it to link your purchase to you so you keep access after reinstalling the app or changing phones.
  • Purchase records. If you buy something in the app, Google Play handles the payment. We receive a confirmation that the purchase happened. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Codes you enter, such as unlock or referral codes, so we can check that they are valid.
  • Your in-app progress, which is stored on your phone.

Ads

The free version may offer optional ads through Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data for ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Paid content is ad-free.

Permissions

The app only asks for the permissions it needs to work. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

How we use and share information

We use the information above only to run the app, give you access to what you have unlocked, and prevent piracy. We do not sell your information. We share it only with services the app runs on (Google Play and, if ads are used, Google AdMob), or if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your email and access records for as long as you have unlocked content. To have your data deleted, email us from the address you used and we will remove it within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is made for students and job seekers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
